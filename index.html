⚡ Session Welcome & Objectives

Welcome! In this lesson, we will explore verbs to make requests and express common daily complaints effectively.

💡 Learning Objective:

By the end of this session, you will accurately use 8 essential two-part verbs to communicate requests, petitions, and roommate complaints in everyday conversations.

📖 Reading: Messy Roommates Report

Living with young adults can be great, but complaints happen! A campus survey shows that 75% of students complain when roommates forget to turn off lights. About 60% get angry when people do not clean up the table after lunch. Around 50% hate it when roommates leave clothes around instead of choosing to hang up sweaters or put away shoes. Finally, 40% expect everyone to take out the trash and throw out old food, while 30% dislike it when people don't pick up scattered items or turn on loud appliances late at night.

1. 75% complain about leaving lights on.

2. 40% complain about cleaning up lunch tables.

3. Half of students dislike unorganized shoes and clothes.
